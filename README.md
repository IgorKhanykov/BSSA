# BSSA
Blind Stop Sieve Algorithm (BSSA)

Citation: Khanykov I.G. Applying the Blind Spot Sieve Algorithm to Create Periodic Tables of
Clustering Methods. - 2026. - 6 p. (unpublished). [Electronic resource]: <https://github.com/IgorKhanykov/BSSA>

This work presents a fundamental result of fifteen years of labor: a method of inquiry that answers the question of *where* to seek truth in the sciences. Together with René Descartes’s method—which addresses the question of *how* to seek truth in the sciences—these two methods form a system of inquiry that guarantees a successful outcome.
