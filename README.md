# BSSA
Blind Spot Sieve Algorithm (BSSA)

Citation: Khanykov I.G. Applying the Blind Spot Sieve Algorithm to Create Periodic Tables of
Clustering Methods. — 2026. — 6 p. (unpublished). [Electronic resource]: <https://github.com/IgorKhanykov/BSSA>

This work presents a fundamental result of fifteen years of scientific labor: a method of inquiry that answers the question of *where* to seek truth in the sciences. Together with René Descartes’s method—which addresses the question of *how* to seek truth in the sciences—these two methods form a system of inquiry that guarantees a successful outcome.

The first section describes the algorithm itself, dubbed the "Sieve of 'Blank Spots'" in homage to Eratosthenes’s Sieve used for identifying prime numbers. The principle behind its application is straightforward: key attributes and their possible values ​​are identified. A matrix is ​​then constructed, and all solutions known to the author are entered at the corresponding intersections. The empty cells represent questions of present interest (pressing problems) for which solutions can—and should—be sought.

The second and third sections of the work present examples of periodic tables filled with with methods known to the author.

In effect, filling in such a periodic table (for instance, Table 3 in this work) is akin to solving a Sudoku puzzle that spans a human lifetime.
